Budget Feature Roadmap

Budget wins where bank-synced apps can't reach: cash in two currencies, loans between family, and a household record nobody can quietly edit. This page sets 23 competitors against the app as built today and the realities of money in Lebanon, then orders what to build next.

Summary

  • The position is already unusual. No app checked combines strict USD/LBP cash wallets, person-to-person loans both ways, private plus household spaces with roles, an uneditable household record, and Arabic/English RTL. YNAB, Monarch and Copilot are single-currency; the multi-currency cash apps convert silently or share loosely, and none documents RTL.
  • Bank sync can't be the answer here, and that's fine. No aggregator covers Lebanon, and broken sync is the category's most common complaint. The job is fast manual entry.
  • Exchange is the biggest local opportunity. None of seven multi-currency apps records "paid $20, got change in lira" as one event. Budget can't record any USD↔LBP exchange today without faking income and expense, which would distort the planned reports.
  • Table stakes still missing: a note or payee on entries, search, recurring bills, export, quick entry, split entries — plus the monthly budget and reports, which are designed but not built.
  • Borrow mechanics, not products: Dollarwise's editable Needs/Wants/Savings split and "safe to spend", YNAB's who-changed-what feed, Actual's confirm-to-post schedules and cash counts, Lunch Money's per-entry rates, Monarch's mine/theirs/ours.
  • Hold off on AI. Assistants are rarely used and "unwanted AI" is a complaint cluster. The one AI idea worth prototyping is a bilingual quick add that drafts an entry for a person to confirm.

Suggested next five milestones

In order. Codes refer to the roadmap below.

  1. Monthly budget, from the existing spec N1
  2. Note and payee on entries X1
  3. USD↔LBP exchange event X2
  4. Reports v1, after exchange exists N2
  5. Quick entry and payee memory X3

Budget today

Built and live in the app: email sign-in and onboarding; personal and household spaces; USD and LBP wallets with derived balances; an immutable journal with four entry shapes and reversals as the only correction; income/expense categories with one level of subcategories; loans in both directions with repayments, due dates and monthly targets; household invitations and roles (email delivery built but not configured); English/Arabic RTL. Monthly budgeting and the reporting read model are fully specified but not built — Reports is a disabled menu item. The public beta is not yet approved for irreplaceable data.

What Budget already does better

Two currencies, never blurred

Monefy counts a missing rate as 1:1, a Wallet user's EUR account turned into CHF, Monarch shows everything as "$". Budget stores exact minor units — USD cents, whole LBP — and never adds them together.

Family loans as a real ledger

Spendee, Toshl, Actual and Copilot have no debt feature; YNAB and Monarch model bank debt. In 2022/23, 58% of Lebanese households sought help from relatives or friends (World Bank).

A record nobody quietly rewrites

Corrections are reversals and every event keeps its actor. Spendee lets any guest delete any entry; Goodbudget and Copilot share one login.

Private and shared, with roles

Rocket Money and Simplifi cap sharing at two people. Monarch still can't budget separately per person.

Arabic and English, right to left

Of the multi-currency apps only Wallet, Money Lover and Bluecoins list Arabic, and none documents RTL. No Lebanese USD/LBP household budgeting app could be verified.

No bank logins to break

Sync failures and re-logins lead the complaints (50+ signals in RightIdea's July 2026 analysis), and bank-data costs drive subscription prices.

  • Change comes back in lira. Consumer prices are almost fully dollarized, yet small dollar notes are scarce, so a $20 note often returns LBP change.
  • Digital rails don't export. Whish Money, OMT Pay and BOB Finance hold USD and LBP with in-app exchange but document no export or API. Byblos, BLOM, Fransabank and BBAC send SMS alerts.
  • Locked deposits linger. Circulars 158/166 allow $1,000 and $500 monthly withdrawals (from December 2025); the deposit "gap law" was still pending in May 2026.
  • Local costs. Generator subscription, EDL electricity, water trucks ($10–22 per 2,000 L), private school fees in fresh dollars, and over $300 of supplies per child.
  • Regional signals. Wafeer parses bank SMS (iOS users paste), Riyalak tracks owed and receivable amounts with reminders, Masarif works offline in full Arabic, and savings-circle apps Hakbah (2M+) and MoneyFellows (8.5M+) peak before Ramadan, Eid and September.

Guardrails every item inherits

These keep new features inside the journal boundaries the repo already enforces.

  1. Drafts are intent; postings are money. Recurring bills, pasted SMS, receipts, imports and offline entries create a non-posting draft. Confirming calls an existing protected command with the draft's request ID. One draft inbox serves all five.

  2. A new event kind is a full-stack change. Exchange, cash-count adjustment, refund and loan write-off each extend the report classification matrix, enum ratchet, reversal rules and command inventory in the same milestone.

  3. Descriptive metadata sits beside the event. Note, payee, tags and attachments use immutable association tables, as Categories v1 did, so verified posting signatures stay unchanged.

  4. A rate belongs to one event. It is derived from the two real amounts, never global state. Any combined total names the rate and date it used.

  5. USD and LBP stay separate in budgets, targets, rollovers, alerts and "left to spend".

  6. Nothing posts itself. No reminder, schedule or suggestion becomes a posting until a member confirms it.

Roadmap

S a bounded read or UI on existing commands · M a new table or command plus UI · L a new event kind touching reversals, reports and loans. Rows are in recommended order within each horizon.

Not for now

ليس الآن

Deliberately left out, with the reason.

  • Bank sync or open banking. No provider covers Lebanon.
  • A live rate feed as truth. Cash deals differ from mid-market; combined totals always name their rate.
  • Editing or deleting posted entries. Or shared logins.
  • AI assistant or acting agent. Rarely used, privacy-heavy, nothing to act on.
  • Machine-learned categorization. Solves a bank-sync problem Budget doesn't have.
  • Age of Money. First-in-first-out per currency for little value.
  • Streaks and gamification. Only vendor evidence.
  • Long-range forecasting. Also investment tracking and bill negotiation.

Worked example: paid in dollars, change in lira

A supermarket bill of LL 1,340,000 is paid with a $20 note and LL 450,000 comes back. Under L1 this is one event with three legs.

LegWalletCur.Minor unitsReported as
Exchange outUSD cashUSD−2000Exchange, not spending
Exchange inLBP cashLBP+1790000Exchange, not income
PurchaseLBP cashLBP−1340000Expense · Groceries
Wallet effect
USD cash −$20.00 · LBP cash +LL 450,000
Rate stored on the event
1,790,000 ÷ 20.00 = 89,500 LBP/USD
Priced in dollars instead
$15 bill, LL 447,500 change → USD −1500 expense, USD −500 and LBP +447500 exchange

Whether legs may share a wallet-movement row is for the design. The invariant: the expense and the exchange are classified separately and reconcile exactly to the two wallet deltas.

Questions that would change this plan

  1. Is Budget staying a private family tool, or becoming a product for Lebanese households? Going public moves onboarding packs, import, pricing and support up.
  2. Should exchange (X2) ship before the Reports UI? Recommended: yes.
  3. Should opt-in rollover (P2) replace "no automatic carry-forward"?
  4. Do household members need a view-only or entry-only role?
  5. May receipt images or SMS text go to an external AI model for drafting?
  6. Are locked bank deposits still part of the households Budget serves?
