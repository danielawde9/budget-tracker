## Two-connection race result

- **Posting vs close:** connection 1 posts a backdated expense (uncommitted);
  connection 2's `close_budget_month` with the earlier preview hash is proven
  blocked on connection 1 (`pg_blocking_pids`), then refused `40001` after
  connection 1 commits; the recomputed preview shows the new fact.
- **Late commit after close:** a transaction that fixed its timestamp before
  the close posts and commits its backdated expense after the close commits;
  its `created_at` is earlier than the close's, and the preview reports
  `restatementRequired: true` with the extra fact.
- **Equal timestamps:** an owner-inserted twin fact copying an existing fact's
  exact `created_at` still changes the digest (`restatementRequired: true`).
- **Concurrent copy on the same expected target head:** one succeeds, the
  blocked second is refused `40001`; exactly one target snapshot exists.
- **Statement timeout:** a close waiting on a held `spaces` row lock with
  `statement_timeout = 300ms` fails `57014` and writes no close.
