# Twice-a-month and last-business-day schedules

**Date:** 2026-09-29. **Base:** `ecf58f7` (`main`). **Branch:** `ws/schedules`.
**Backlog:** item 4 ("Bills that repeat twice a month or on the last working day").

## Cadence semantics (owner decision)

Two new cadences on `schedule_revisions`, alongside `weekly` / `monthly` / `yearly`:

- **`semimonthly`** — two occurrences per month, on the **1st and the 15th**. `interval_count` MUST be `1` (rejected otherwise).
- **`monthly_last_business_day`** — monthly on the **last Monday–Friday** of the month; `interval_count` is the number of months between occurrences (1–12), exactly like `monthly`.
- "Business day" = Monday–Friday. There is **no** holiday calendar.

## Remaining limitations

- Business days exclude public holidays (no holiday calendar exists in the product).
- `semimonthly` is fixed to the 1st and 15th (not user-chosen days).
- Occurrence identity is still `(schedule_id, due_date)`, so switching a schedule's
  cadence re-materializes new due dates and leaves already-generated ones in place
  (the existing occurrence rows are not rewritten).
