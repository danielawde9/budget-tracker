# w4a-1 — per-space timezone + one server clock

**Date:** 2026-09-28. **Base:** `a8346a4` (`main`). **Branch:** `ws/w4a-clock`.
**Audit:** `docs/verification/2026-09-25-linking-audit.md` rank 11, §3.5 (E1, E2a, A7, D12) and §6.
**Contracts:** `docs/superpowers/plans/future-planning/00-start-here.md`, `01-sql-contract.md`, `46-pay-date-db.md` (context).

## Delivered

The app had two clocks: Home's/Plan's month and the record date were browser-local,
while "today", confirm and auto-settle were UTC (`(now() at time zone 'UTC')::date`).
In Beirut from 00:00-03:00 the month and the as-of date disagreed, so a bill paid
then failed to link and stayed reserved.

- **Migration** `supabase/migrations/20260928100000_space_timezone.sql` (forward-only,
  version > `20260927100000`): `spaces.timezone` (text, NOT NULL, default `'UTC'`)
  with a validating trigger against `pg_timezone_names`; `private.space_today(uuid)`
  (the production form anchored on `now()` in the space zone) and `public.space_today`,
  `public.space_clock(uuid)` (timezone + today + currentMonth); and forward-fixes the
  UTC-today sites on the reads the client consumes (`available_cash_summary`,
  `cash_outlook`, `scheduled_occurrence_page`, `scheduled_overdue_page`).
- **Client** `src/features/workspace/space-clock.ts`: the workspace publishes the
  selected space's server clock to a module-level store; `routes.tsx` derives the
  month, the cash-control as-of date, the occurrence window and the unlink date from
  it, and `confirm-payment-dialog.tsx` / `record-sheet.tsx` take their date default
  and "today or earlier" bound from it. The browser-local `currentMonthStart()` and
  the duplicated UTC `todayIso()` are gone from the Control Room.
- **Tests**: `tests/db/space-timezone.integration.test.ts` (6 cases over the offset
  boundary), the rewritten `src/features/control-room/local-vs-utc-clock.test.tsx`,
  and the updated workspace/control-room/recurring tests.

Payday anchoring is deliberately **not** in this slice (tracked as W4a-2).

## Coordinator completion (the sub-agent hit its turn limit twice)

The agent delivered the migration, the helper, the client clock and the unit tests,
then ran out of turns mid-way through the browser pass. The coordinator finished:

- two failing unit tests that did not publish the space clock
  (`occurrence-detail.test.tsx`, `overdue-actions.test.tsx`);
- three e2e fixture gaps the new contract exposed: `spaces.timezone` was missing from
  the space rows in `e2e/fixtures/loans.ts` (two rows, plus the `create_space` row) and
  `e2e/fixtures/household.ts`, and neither fixture stubbed the new `space_clock` RPC —
  so `listSpaces` threw and the whole shell failed to render. With those fixed the
  suite went from 7 passed/163 failed to 149 passed with only the household spec still
  red, then green.

## Remaining limitations

- **W4a-2 (payday anchoring) is not done.** The month window is still the calendar
  month; the period key is unchanged.
- `useToday()` returns `''` if the clock is not yet published (the shell only renders
  once the workspace is ready and the clock is set, but the empty string is a hazard
  worth tightening).
- No dedicated e2e spec asserts the single-clock behaviour; the DB test and the
  rewritten unit test are the net.
- `pnpm check:ops`' migration tests were expected to fail here (the manifest was still
  56) until the coordinator's release-prep commit regenerated it to 57.
